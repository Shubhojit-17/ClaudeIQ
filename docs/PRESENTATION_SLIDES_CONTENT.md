### Slide 2: The Enterprise Legal Challenge (Problem Statement)
- **Massive Contract Volume**: Modern enterprises sign thousands of multi-page MSAs, NDAs, DPAs, and SLA contracts annually across global vendors.
- **Critical Risk of Blindspots**: Hidden indemnification traps, auto-renewal penalty traps, unlimited liability clauses, and missing GDPR/DPA obligations.
- **The "AI Hallucination" Liability**: Generic chatbots (ChatGPT, generic LLMs) paraphrase or fabricate legal conditions, creating catastrophic compliance and lawsuit liabilities.
- **Access & Security Governance Gap**: Junior reviewers or viewers frequently see unauthorized sensitive terms or lack immutable auditability when agreements are modified or signed.

---

### Slide 3: The ClauseIQ Solution (Value Proposition)
- **Zero-Hallucination Assurance**: Every extracted clause, risk flag, and obligation is tied to an exact, verbatim character-level quote from the original source contract.
- **Plain-English Clause Translation**: Translates complex legal legalese into instant plain-English executive summaries beneath every clause.
- **Multi-Clause Topic Comparison**: Automatically cross-correlates interlocking clauses (e.g., matching a Data Protection clause against a Confidentiality or Breach Notification provision).
- **Admin-Gated Legal Execution**: Official contract signing workflow with cryptographic timestamps and role-based access control (RBAC).
- **Hierarchical Security Audit Trail**: Tamper-evident logging of every ingestion, exploration, and signing event.

---

### Slide 4: System Architecture & Technical Foundation
- **Backend Architecture**: High-performance FastAPI server with async concurrency, RESTful endpoints, and Pydantic v2 schemas.
- **Database Layer**: SQLAlchemy ORM with custom cross-platform GUID type decorators (PostgreSQL production / SQLite local) and Row-Level Security (RLS) filtering.
- **AI Inference Engine**: Pluggable multi-provider architecture supporting:
  - **Groq Cloud** (Llama 3.3 70B • ~500 tokens/sec high-throughput extraction)
  - **Google Gemini** (Gemini 2.0 Flash)
  - **OpenAI** (GPT-4o / GPT-4o-mini)
  - **Verbatim Rule Engine** (Deterministic fallback if cloud connectivity is offline)
- **Frontend Layer**: React 18 + Vite, TailwindCSS cyber design system, Plus Jakarta Sans & JetBrains Mono typography, frosted glassmorphism, and live telemetry widgets.

---

### Slide 5: Core Workflow & Ingestion Pipeline
1. **Document Ingestion**: Multi-format parser (PDF, DOCX, TXT) with OCR and layout-preserving token extraction.
2. **Semantic Boundary Indexing**: Intelligent chunking dividing agreements into operative numbered clauses.
3. **Automated Risk Scoring**: Multi-tier threat classification (High / Medium / Low / Compliant) checking indemnification, liability caps, warranties, and termination terms.
4. **Obligation Extraction**: Milestone deadline parsing with responsible party assignments and alert triggers.
5. **Executive AI Synthesis**: High-level contract overview summarizing core commitments and critical exposure areas.

### Slide 13: Enterprise Security, RBAC & Multi-Tenancy
- **Strict Role-Based Hierarchy**:
  - **Administrator** (`admin`): Full organization control, contract signing & execution authority, global audit trail inspection.
  - **Legal Reviewer** (`reviewer`): Ingestion, clause exploration, risk annotation, scoped audit trail access.
  - **Business Viewer** (`viewer`): Read-only contract intelligence, obligation tracking, self-activity ledger.
- **Row-Level Security (RLS)**: Enforced directly at the SQL database layer to prevent cross-tenant data leaks.
- **Zero-Storage Retention for AI APIs**: API keys are encrypted and external LLMs are utilized strictly for stateless zero-retention inference.
