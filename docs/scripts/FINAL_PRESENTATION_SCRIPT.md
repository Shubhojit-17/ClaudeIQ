## 🎙️ Speaker 1: Shubhojit Sarkar
**Slides**: *1 (Cover) → 2 (Snapshot) → 3 (Problem)* | **Time**: ~50 seconds

> **[Slide 1: Cover]**  
> *"Good morning respected judges. We are team **DarkTrace** from **PES University**, presenting **ClauseIQ** — an Autonomous AI Contract Intelligence and Compliance Assistant."*

> **[Slide 2: Snapshot]**  
> *"In one line: **ClauseIQ turns slow, risky manual contract review into fast, defensible, AI-assisted workflows.**  
> It reads multi-page agreements in seconds, flags high-risk clauses, tracks critical renewal dates, and translates dense legal jargon into plain English for legal, procurement, and finance teams."*

> **[Slide 3: Problem]**  
> *"Today, corporate contracts pile up faster than legal teams can read them.  
> Manual reviews take hours, a single missed auto-renewal or uncapped liability can cost lakhs, and generic AI tools like raw ChatGPT hallucinate legal clauses — creating dangerous liabilities.  
> The real challenge isn't just speed — it is **verbatim traceability and defensible decisions**.  
> **Khadeeja** will now explain our solution and architecture."*

## 🎙️ Speaker 2: Shaik Khadeeja Sayeed
**Slides**: *4 (Solution) → 5 (Architecture) → 6 (Pipeline)* | **Time**: ~50 seconds

> **[Slide 4: Solution]**  
> *"Thank you, Shubhojit. ClauseIQ solves this through five capabilities:  
> - **100% Verbatim Grounding**: Every clause and risk cites the exact, untouched source quote. Zero hallucinations.  
> - **Plain-English Translation**: Executive summaries right beneath each clause.  
> - **Topic Comparison**: Cross-referencing overlapping terms across clauses.  
> - **Admin-Gated Execution**: Legal signing workflow with cryptographic timestamps.  
> - **Tamper-Evident Audit Logging**: Tracking every user action."*

> **[Slide 5 & 6: Architecture & Pipeline]**  
> *"Technically, we built a layered system:  
> A **React 18 cyber dashboard** on the frontend, an async **FastAPI** backend, and an **SQLAlchemy ORM** with Row-Level Security.  
> Our AI engine is multi-provider pluggable — running **Groq Llama 3.3 70B** at 500 tokens/sec, **Gemini 2.0 Flash**, and **GPT-4o**, backed by a deterministic offline rule engine.  
> In 5 automated steps: it **Ingests** the PDF, **Indexes** clauses, **Analyzes** risk, **Tracks** deadlines, and **Synthesizes** an executive view.  
> Over to **Shreeya** for the product experience."*

## 🎙️ Speaker 3: Shreeya Nagaraj
**Slides**: *7 (Dashboard) → 8 (Clause Explorer) → 9 (Topic Matrix)* | **Time**: ~55 seconds

> **[Slide 7: Command Center Dashboard]**  
> *"Thank you, Khadeeja. This is the **ClauseIQ Command Center**:  
> At the top, live telemetry cards monitor active contract repositories, flagged risks, upcoming milestone dates, and an overall compliance health score. Reviewers can instantly filter and search across all ingested contracts."*

> **[Slide 8: Grounded Clause Explorer]**  
> *"Inside any contract, the **Clause Explorer** shows:  
> First, an overarching AI Executive Summary.  
> Second, beneath each clause, a dedicated **Plain-English Summary** so non-lawyers instantly understand their obligations.  
> And third, our **Exact Verbatim Citation box** — proving the exact source text behind every AI conclusion with zero hallucination."*

> **[Slide 9: Multi-Clause Topic Matrix]**  
> *"In real contracts, obligations are scattered across different pages.  
> In our **Topic Matrix**, ClauseIQ automatically groups interlocking clauses under topics like *Confidentiality* or *Data Protection*, running an AI Comparative Synthesis to spot conflicts side-by-side in seconds.  
> **Sindhu** will now walk through our Risk Radar and Signing flow."*

## 🎙️ Speaker 4: Sindhu S B
**Slides**: *10 (Risk & Milestones) → 11 (Signing) → 12 (Walkthrough Video)* | **Time**: ~55 seconds

> **[Slide 10: Risk Radar & Milestones]**  
> *"Thank you, Shreeya.  
> In our **Risk Radar**, agreements are filtered by High, Medium, and Low risk severity — targeting traps like uncapped indemnification or short termination windows, each backed by verbatim citations.  
> In the **Milestones Tracker**, upcoming deadlines, audit windows, and auto-renewals are mapped to designated owners with countdown alert triggers."*

> **[Slide 11: Enterprise Signing & Custom Cyber Modal]**  
> *"When a contract is ready to execute:  
> Only verified **Administrators** hold signing authority.  
> We built a custom **Cyber Confirmation Modal** that displays the signing impact and administrative credentials. Once confirmed, ClauseIQ stamps the agreement as **'Executed & Sealed'** with an immutable UTC audit record. Lower roles receive a security access block."*

> **[Slide 12: Live Walkthrough Video]**  
> *"Our recorded video demonstration shows this entire flow live: from uploading an agreement, extracting grounded clauses, running topic synthesis, to executing the contract under the Admin persona.  
> I will now invite **Deepika** to conclude."*
